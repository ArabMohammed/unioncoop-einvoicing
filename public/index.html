Introduction of Electronic Invoicing (“e-invoicing”) in the United Arab Emirates

The UAE Ministry of Finance will implement mandatory e-invoicing from 1 January 2027 for B2B and B2G transactions. To exchange compliant e-invoices with your organisation through the Peppol network, we need to hold certain mandatory data about your entity.

Read the full notice

This follows Federal Decree-Law No. 16 of 2024 and Federal Decree-Law No. 17 of 2024, which amend the VAT Law and the Tax Procedures Law respectively to introduce e-invoicing requirements, and Ministerial Decisions No. 243 and No. 244 of 2025, which establish the Electronic Invoicing System and its implementation timeline. Businesses are required to go live in phases, based on predefined revenue thresholds.

Union Coop, as a responsible taxpayer, has already begun taking the necessary steps to comply. From go-live, e-invoices will be exchanged in structured XML format via our Accredited Service Provider (ASP), fulfilling all requirements of a UAE tax invoice. The fields below will appear on every electronic invoice exchanged with you.

Please complete this form within two weeks of receiving our notice. It takes about 5 minutes — your progress is saved on this device automatically.
